How it works

Canny finds boundaries. The tool traces long, mostly vertical edges and removes normal tilt and simple bowing. It looks for nonlinear bending that agrees across separate parts of the image.

Scores are heuristic indices, not probabilities. Curved architecture or vegetation can resemble distortion. Sparse scenes can hide it. Validate thresholds on images from your camera.

Blur is not scored. Missing or broken edges reduce the available evidence.
